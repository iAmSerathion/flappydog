2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Sky Pup is a casual one-tap arcade game. The player guides a small winged dog through gaps between bone pillars; each gap scores a point and the best score is saved on the device. It is meant for everyone (rated 4+), including children and older or less experienced players: buttons are large and clearly labelled, and a Relaxed mode offers wider gaps and a slower pace. The value is a short, calm game for spare minutes that works offline, with no ads, no purchases, no account and no data collection.

3. INSTRUCTIONS
No login, setup or sample files are needed.
- Launch the app and tap "Play".
- Tap anywhere on the screen to start, then tap to make the dog flap upwards. Avoid the pillars, the ground and the top of the screen.
- "Pause" (top right) pauses the game; "Resume" or "Home" continue.
- After a crash the game-over card shows score and best score; "Play again" restarts.
- "Settings" on the start screen: turn sound and haptics on or off, and switch on Relaxed mode (keeps its own best score).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app makes no network connections and contains no third-party SDKs: no analytics, no advertising, no authentication, no payment processor, no AI services and no data providers. All graphics are drawn in code and all sounds were created by us; scores and settings are stored only on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region. The in-game text is English; there is no region-specific content or feature.
