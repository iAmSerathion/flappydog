## Description

Sky Pup is a cozy one-tap arcade game. Guide a round little dog with feathered wings through the gaps between bone pillars, high above a park of clouds and rolling hills.

- One control: tap anywhere to flap.
- Every gap you clear scores a point. Beat your best score.
- Relaxed mode: wider gaps and a slower pace, with its own best score.
- Big, clearly labelled buttons that are easy to hit.
- Sound and haptics you can switch off.
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking. Works offline.
