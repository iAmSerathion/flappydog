# Privacy Policy – Sky Pup

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Sky Pup does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- It makes no network connections.
- Your best scores and settings (sound, haptics, Relaxed mode) are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
